Open the design canvas (copy the address above if the link does not open from here).

The line colours (U1, U3, U2, S1) and the stop name "Portagem" are placeholders. The real values come from the Metrobus stop and route data. The map is illustrative; the app uses MapLibre on OpenStreetMap tiles.

Map: nearest stop (phone)

The home screen. The map is centred on your location, zoomed to frame the nearest stop and the nearest bus. The bottom sheet shows the stop, its distance and its lines, then the nearest bus, clearly marked as scheduled.

Finding your location (phone)

Shown while the app waits for a location fix or for the browser's permission prompt. It shows the default view over Coimbra, with placeholders where the nearest stop will appear. The locate button is disabled.

Location unavailable (phone)

Shown when location permission is denied or the device can't get a fix. The map stays usable over Coimbra, and a non-blocking sheet explains why the nearest stop is missing and offers "Try again".
