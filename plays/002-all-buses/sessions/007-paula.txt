  ⎿ 60	## Nearest approaching bus
  ⎿ 61	
  ⎿ 62	`nearestApproachingBus` in `nearestBus.ts` works as follows:
  ⎿ 63	
  ⎿ 64	1. **Candidates.** A candidate is a trip, on today's service day (or on yesterday's before
  ⎿ 65	   04:00), that **has already started** and **has not yet reached** the nearest stop. Trips that
  ⎿ 66	   have not left their first stop are ignored, even if they would arrive sooner than any bus
  ⎿ 67	   already running.
  ⎿ 68	2. **Choice.** The candidate with the soonest scheduled arrival at the stop wins.
  ⎿ 69	3. **Position.** The bus is placed along its line's shape. Each of the trip's stops is projected
  ⎿ 70	   onto the shape in order, and each projection only searches the shape from the previous stop's
  ⎿ 71	   segment onwards, so the distances never decrease. This keeps stops that sit beside the line,
  ⎿ 72	   such as the asc/desc pairs, on the correct stretch. The distance along the shape is then
  ⎿ 73	   interpolated linearly in time between the scheduled stop times on either side of now. Stop
  ⎿ 74	   distances are memoised for each shape and stop sequence.
  ⎿ 75	4. **No bus.** The result is `null` when there is no candidate, for example late at night or at a
  ⎿ 76	   terminus with no trip in progress.
  ⎿ 77	
  ⎿ 78	The result is a `BusPosition` that includes `arrivalAtStopSeconds`, the time until the scheduled
  ⎿ 79	arrival. This is the basis for a future "minutes away" display, which the UI does not show yet.
