● Right now the app shows **only one bus**: the one due at your nearest stop soonest.

- `nearestApproachingBus` in `src/domain/nearestBus.ts` goes through all of today's trips, keeps the ones that have started but haven't reached your stop yet, and returns just the one arriving first. It returns `null` if none qualify.
- The map screen draws one bus marker for that result. The sheet shows a single "Nearest bus · U1" row with a **Scheduled** badge (see `documentation/modules/map-screen.md`).
- Buses that haven't left their first stop are skipped, even if one would reach you sooner than a bus already on the road.

That falls short of the product vision. `documentation/context/vision.md` says the map should show "the live position of **every** Metrobus bus, and how many minutes each approaching bus is from that stop." Two things are missing:

1. **All buses on the map.** Only one is drawn today.
2. **"Minutes away".** `arrivalAtStopSeconds` is already calculated, but the UI doesn't display it yet.

If you want to close that gap, it would make a good next play. The estimation logic already works out a position for any trip in progress, so showing every bus mostly means running it for all active trips instead of just the soonest one.
