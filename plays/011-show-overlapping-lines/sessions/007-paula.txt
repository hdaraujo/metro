- **Map → Layers bullet:** now names the `routes` layer with the `stops` layer drawn above it, and links to the new section.
- **New "Route lines" section:** explains how lines that share a road are drawn side by side.
  - **How it's computed:** the pure function `bundleRoutes` in `src/domain/routeBundles.ts`. Two lines overlap only where their vertices are exactly identical; anywhere else they are drawn on the road centre, so they overlap as before.
  - **Per line, not per direction:** a line's two directions are drawn as one strand, and each piece of road is drawn once per line.
  - **Order across the road:** lines are ordered by id across the road, the bundle is centred on it, and each line keeps its side along a shared stretch.
  - **Drawing:** strands are 4 px wide and touch with no gap. A line steps sideways slightly where it joins or leaves a bundle; that step is accepted.
  - **What stays on the road centre:** stop circles and bus markers. Buses are still placed on the original route shapes, which I checked in `src/domain/buses.ts`.
  - **Tests:** they check which side of the road each line is on, not the internal direction choice, so that choice can change without rewriting them.
