- **New `src/domain/routeBundles.ts`:** `bundleRoutes(shapes)` returns `LineRun[]`, each a stretch of one line with its colour, a sideways offset and its points. It follows the plan's algorithm.
  - **Overlap:** two shapes overlap on a stretch where they share exactly the same consecutive points.
  - **Order:** lines are ranked by name (S1, S2, U1, U2, U3), and that sets their left-to-right order wherever they share a stretch.
  - **Sides:** each line keeps the same side along a shared stretch, even where shapes were drawn in opposite directions.
  - **No double drawing:** each line draws each stretch once, so its outbound and return shapes no longer overlap each other.
  - Repeated points and shapes with fewer than two points are handled. The output is always the same for the same input.
- **New `src/domain/routeBundles.test.ts`:** 8 tests. They cover the plan's synthetic cases (one line, shared stretch, opposite directions, lines joining and leaving, bad input, input order) and the recorded network. On the recorded data, the lines on each stretch get distinct, centred positions, every stretch is covered once per line, and at least one stretch carries 4 or more lines.
