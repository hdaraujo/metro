1. **At most 4 buses:** under "Heading to this stop" there are never more than 4 rows, and no "+N more heading here" line.

6. **Buses behind the sheet (the bug fix):** pan the map so an approaching bus (a highlighted marker) slides under the bottom sheet or the "Metro" wordmark. The bus must be hidden beneath them, not drawn on top.
7. **Locate button:** with the sheet minimised, tap "Centre on my location". The map frames the stop and buses using the smaller sheet's height.
