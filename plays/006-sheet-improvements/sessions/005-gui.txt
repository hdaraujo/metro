● I've implemented play 006 in the `metro` worktree, following the plan with two small deviations described below. Lint, typecheck, `npm test` (95 tests), `npm run build` and the full Playwright suite (19 passed, 5 skipped because they are for the other layout) all pass. I didn't do the manual phone-viewport check from the plan. Nothing is committed, and nothing under `documentation/` was touched.

**The four changes:**
- **Buses in front of the sheet:** the map is now its own layer (`z-index: 0` on `.map`), so a bus can't draw over the sheet, the wordmark or the desktop panel. Approaching buses still draw above the other buses. With the fix removed, the new test "buses draw beneath the sheet" fails on both phone and desktop. With it, the test passes.
- **Four buses:** the list stops at 4 rows and the "+N more heading here" line is gone. I pulled the row out into a reusable `BusRow` component, which can carry its own Scheduled badge.
- **Bottom label:** the "Estimated at…" note is removed. A short `Timetable data from {date} may be out of date.` line shows only when the timetable is over 24 hours old, in both the full and minimised sheet.
- **Minimising on phones:**
  - The grab handle is now a button (Minimise / Expand), and swiping down or up on the sheet does the same.
  - Minimised, the sheet shows the stop name, its distance and the next bus with its Scheduled badge. The other states (locating, location off, loading error) keep only their title row.
  - The sheet always opens expanded. The desktop panel never collapses and has no toggle.

**Where I departed from the plan:**
- **Mouse swipes:** the plan listened for the finger lifting only on the sheet itself. With a mouse, a swipe up that ended over the map was missed, so the sheet now listens on the whole window.
- **Text selection:** the phone sheet no longer allows selecting text. In testing, one swipe selected the text and the next swipe started dragging it instead of moving the sheet.
- **Handle spacing:** because the minimised sheet has tighter spacing, I added one CSS rule to keep its content starting at the same place below the handle.

**Tests:** the existing sheet test now checks that the old note and the "+N more" line are gone. New tests cover minimising with the button, swiping on the phone, no toggle on desktop, and buses drawing beneath the sheet.

I ran `npm ci` in the worktree because it had no dependencies installed.
