Removing the label is different: it clashes with one of the vision's principles, so I need your decision first.

**The clash.** The vision says estimated data is "clearly labelled 'scheduled'" and "never shown as live". Right now every bus marker on the map has a "Scheduled" tag under it, and the tag holds that bus's countdown (e.g. `3:04 Scheduled`). The tag was put there so a countdown on the map can't be mistaken for a live time.

The intent can still be met honestly, because the screen already marks estimates in other places:
- a single **Scheduled** badge on the "Heading to this stop" list in the sheet;
- the note under that list ("Positions and times estimated from the timetable…");
- the **dashed border** on every bus marker, which is how the design marks an estimated position.

**What I'd change.** I'd reword only that one principle in `vision.md`, to say that estimates must be clearly marked on screen but each bus doesn't need its own label:

> **Real time, honestly labelled.** Bus positions and "minutes away" update continuously. Until a live feed exists, they are estimated from the timetable and clearly marked as scheduled: a visible label covers them, and a scheduled bus always looks different from a live one, though it need not carry its own label. Live data replaces the estimates when it becomes available. Estimated or stale data is never shown as live.

`tech.md` stays as it is: its rule that "the UI always shows which one it is" still holds.

The Planner will settle whether the map countdown next to each approaching bus stays once the tag is gone.
