- **Bus markers:** No bus on the map shows the word "Scheduled" any more. A bus heading to the nearest stop shows only its countdown under the pill (for example `3:04`). Other buses have no tag. The doc notes three things a future implementer could trip over:
  - The dashed border and the sheet's single Scheduled badge are now the only visible sign that a position is estimated. While location is off or still being found, the map's buses have only the border.
  - The tag element stays in every marker and is hidden when empty. It needs a CSS rule (`.marker-bus__tag[hidden] { display: none; }`), or it draws an empty yellow box.
  - Each marker's screen-reader label still says "Scheduled", and it has to stay that way.
- **Default zoom:** The first frame shows you, the nearest stop, and the next approaching bus in each direction, so up to two buses. A stop served in one direction only frames one bus, and a stop with no approaching bus frames just you and the stop.
