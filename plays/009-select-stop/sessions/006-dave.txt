### 3. What to check

   - The red stop marker moves to Parque.
   - Buses heading to Parque get countdown tags, and every other bus is dimmed.
2. **Tap it again after panning.** It re-frames on the selected stop.
3. **Tap empty map.** The sheet goes back to `// NEAREST STOP` (Portagem) and the map goes back to the default framing: you, Portagem and the soonest bus each way.
4. **Tap empty map with nothing selected.** Nothing happens: the sheet and the camera stay as they are.
5. **Drag or pan the map.** Panning never selects or deselects a stop.
6. **Tap your nearest stop (Portagem) while another stop is selected.** The sheet shows `// NEAREST STOP`, not "Selected stop".
7. **Locate button.** Select a stop, then press "Centre on my location". The app goes back to the nearest stop and the default framing.
8. **Direction reset.** Select a stop that is served both ways, such as Portagem, cycle the direction button to one direction, then select another stop. The direction goes back to "Both directions".
9. **Location off.** In the site settings (the lock icon → Location → Block), block location and reload. The sheet says location is off.
   - Tap Parque: its buses and countdowns appear, and there is no "away" distance.
   - Tap empty map: "Location is off" comes back, and the camera doesn't move.
10. **Desktop only.** The mouse cursor becomes a pointer over a stop circle and goes back to normal over empty map.

**Accepted limitations** (out of scope for this play): double-clicking empty map to zoom also counts as tapping away when a stop is selected, and stops can't be selected with the keyboard.
