● I updated the "Route lines" section of `documentation/modules/map-screen.md` instead of adding a new document, because that section already covers this part of the product. The code matched the plan, so the document describes what was actually built.

The section now covers:
- **One strand per line:** before the map works out which roads lines share, each inbound vertex is moved onto the nearest vertex of the same line's outbound route if it is within `DIRECTION_MERGE_METERS` (75 m). As a result, each line is drawn as one strand for both directions.
- **Why it was needed:** in the city centre, S2, U2 and U3 stop at paired stops on opposite sides of the street (Câmara, Mercado, República). Without the merge, that stretch showed six strands instead of three.
- **Why 75 m, and when to revisit it:** the farthest pair is about 63 m apart, and the closest pair of vertices from different lines is about 104 m apart. Lines are only ever merged with themselves, never with another line.
- **Limits worth knowing:** the merge only changes how lines are drawn. The original route data is untouched, the result does not depend on input order, and a line with no outbound route is left as is.
- **Side effect:** stop circles and bus markers still use real coordinates. On the centre stretch, the stops on the inbound side and the buses going that way sit beside the strand rather than on it: about 5 m off at Câmara and Mercado, and about 63 m at República.
- **What the tests now check on the recorded data:** no line appears twice on a road, at most 4 lines share one road, every outbound road is drawn, and only S2, U2 and U3 appear between Câmara and República.
