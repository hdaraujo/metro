● The timetable data isn't changing. The problem is in the app: the list only includes buses that have **already left their first stop**, so a bus appears only once its trip starts, even when it's due at your stop before buses already on the list.

## The data is stable
- I downloaded `stops.json`, `route-shapes.json` and `trips-{DU,Sab,Dom}.json` three times. Every copy was byte-identical, and the weekday trips still match the fixtures recorded on 25 Sep.
- Trip ids are unique, every stop name a trip uses exists, and there are no unknown directions. The parser isn't dropping anything.
- The service worker's stale-while-revalidate caching isn't the cause either, because there is nothing newer for it to swap in.

## What's wrong
`src/domain/buses.ts:91`:
```ts
if (!first || !last || first.seconds > offset || offset > last.seconds) continue;
```
`busesInService` skips any trip that hasn't reached its first departure time yet, and the sheet's list is built from its output. That was meant for placing buses on the map, since a trip that hasn't started has no position to show. But it also means the list never shows a bus that's still waiting at its terminus.

I replayed the real weekday timetable through the same rule. "App" is the current list; "timetable" is what the next 4 arrivals really are:

| Stop @ time | App shows | Missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Casa do Sal @ 08:00 | S1 1m, U3 6m, S1 16m, **S1 36m** | U3 → República in **6m** (leaves Coimbra B 08:05), U3 → Coimbra B in **10m** (leaves República 08:02) |
| Casa do Sal @ 08:10 | S1 6m, S1 26m, S1 47m | U1 in **6m**, S1 → Serpins in **11m**, U3 in **12m** |
| Serpins @ 08:00 | 2 buses | the 08:20 S1 and the 08:50 S2 departures |

This matches what you saw: open the app at 08:00, then again at 08:03 after the U3 has left Coimbra B, and a bus arriving sooner than ones already listed "appears". It also happens while the app stays open, since the list recalculates every second. Over every stop in 5-minute steps from 06:00 to 22:00 on a weekday, the list's first bus was missing or wrong in **12.1%** of cases (882 of 7,296).

## Two related side effects
1. **End-of-line stops only list buses arriving to finish there.** At Coimbra B at 08:00, the app shows 4 buses, all ending at Coimbra B. The departures you could actually board (U1 → Vale das Flores 08:15, S1 → Serpins 08:20) never appear, because a trip is never "heading to" its own first stop. `directionsAtStop` (`src/domain/directions.ts:22`) also ignores first-stop calls, so the direction filter at these stops is affected in the same way.
2. The list is cut to 4 rows (`src/ui/sheet/ApproachingBuses.tsx:8`), so a bus 36 minutes away can fill a row that belongs to one 6 minutes away.

## How to fix it
Build the list from the timetable, separately from which buses are on the map:
- The sheet lists the next scheduled calls at the stop, including trips that haven't started (optionally within a time limit). At an end-of-line stop, that includes buses leaving from there, and buses that finish there are probably left out.
- The map keeps showing only buses in service, or shows a not-yet-started bus at its first stop marked "departs 08:15".
- A new test covers a stop where the soonest bus hasn't left its first stop yet.

**What changed**
- **Which buses are listed:** the sheet now lists every trip whose next stop at your stop is within the next 90 minutes, including trips still waiting at their terminus. The map is unchanged: a bus only gets a marker once it has actually left, since before that there's no position to show. The new function is `upcomingArrivals` in `src/domain/buses.ts`.

The direction button follows the same rule, so at a terminus it now reflects the direction you can actually leave in.
- **Why 90 minutes:** no trip takes longer than about 70 minutes, so every bus already on its way still makes the list. Without a limit, at 01:00 it would show the first morning bus as `300:00`.

**Things you'll notice**
- A bus that hasn't left yet looks the same in the list as any other, with a countdown to when it reaches your stop. So the top row can be a bus that isn't on the map yet, and the automatic zoom still only includes buses already on their way.
